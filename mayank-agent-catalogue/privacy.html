Mayank Deals — Privacy Notice

When you submit an enquiry, the details you provide (name, phone, email, model, storage, colour, city, query and campaign source) are sent privately to the Mayank Deals sales inbox by a server-side form handler.

The public website does not display the business inbox address and does not provide a public page for viewing customer enquiries. The information is used to respond to your enquiry and arrange follow-up about that request.

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card details or other sensitive credentials. Submitting an enquiry is not an order or payment confirmation.

You can ask the business to stop contacting you about your enquiry.
