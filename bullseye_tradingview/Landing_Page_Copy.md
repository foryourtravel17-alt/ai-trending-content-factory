## Who this is for
Beginners and developing chart readers who want a written process to practice and review. The course teaches definitions and repeatable analysis—not instant results.

## The five pillars
- **SCOPE:** distinguish bullish, bearish and unclear higher-timeframe context.
- **TARGET:** define a pullback zone using EMA 21 and ATR(14).
- **TRIGGER:** use defined candle criteria and a confirmed close.
- **SHIELD:** plan the stop reference and understand sizing limits.
- **BULLSEYE:** understand 1R and 2R planning in a historical research framework.
- **GUARDRAILS:** session window, daily cap, news awareness, journaling and honest testing.
