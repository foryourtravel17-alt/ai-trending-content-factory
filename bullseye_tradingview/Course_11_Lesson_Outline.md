# Bullseye Theory — 11-Lesson Course Outline v0.2

1. **Charts for Beginners** — price chart, symbol, timeframe, candles, OHLC and spread; reading charts without placing trades.
2. **Market Structure and Neutral Conditions** — higher highs/lows, lower highs/lows, ranges and why FLAT is a valid outcome.
3. **SCOPE: Direction First** — H4 for FX/crypto and Daily for stocks; EMA 50 vs EMA 200; completed higher-timeframe bars.
4. **Trend Filters Can Fail** — moving-average lag, ranging markets, false context, and examples that invalidate a setup.
5. **TARGET: Pullback Zone** — EMA 21 ± 0.5 ATR(14); zone touch vs chasing and how volatility changes the band.
6. **Candlestick Trigger Basics** — candle anatomy, engulfing body percentage and rejection/pin bars.
7. **TRIGGER: Confirmation** — candle intersects the zone and closes outside it in the scope direction; why unconfirmed candles are not final.
8. **SHIELD: Risk Boundary** — stop reference 1.5 ATR beyond zone, risk distance, 1% risk concept, gaps and contract-size caveats.
9. **BULLSEYE: 1R / 2R** — partial 50% at 1R, rest at 2R, original stop remains in place at 1R in this ruleset.
10. **Backtesting Honestly** — realistic costs, sample size, drawdown, expectancy, overfitting and out-of-sample testing.
11. **Discipline and Journal** — max 2 setups/day, 12:00–20:00 IST, high-impact news blackout and when not to act.

Each lesson needs plain-English explanation, a historical/non-live chart illustration, exercise, short quiz and a "what can go wrong" section. Do not claim a lesson is recorded or published until those files exist and are reviewed.
