## TradingView commercial policy — checked 23 Oct 2026
- Open-source scripts show source code. Open and Protected scripts are always free.
- Protected hides source code but is available only to authors with a paid plan.
- Invite-only requires Premium or higher. TradingView's 30 Sep 2026 announcement says commercial invite-only access sales—including access bundled with a paid course/service—move to its Marketplace from **1 Nov 2026**; it advises vendors to contact `creators@tradingview.com` before the deadline.
- A standalone educational course can be sold independently, but do not promise paid invite-only indicator access or sell/bundle it outside the permitted route.
- For a free-first launch, verify TradingView's response first. A genuinely free public open-source indicator plus an independently sold educational course may be the simpler route, but it means the code is visible.
