# Mayank iPhone Stock — catalogue site

This is a static catalogue and short customer enquiry page. It has no custom domain requirement and can be hosted on a free provider subdomain.

## Lead delivery
The form submits to FormSubmit and sends the enquiry to mayanksharmaa1201@gmail.com. On the first real/test submission, FormSubmit may send a verification email to that inbox. Open it and click Activate. After activation, new enquiries are emailed to the business inbox. FormSubmit is a third-party service; its delivery and privacy terms apply. No Google Form or Google Sheet is required for this first version.
