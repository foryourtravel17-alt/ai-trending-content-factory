Privacy note

When you submit the enquiry form, the details you provide (name, phone number, requested model, storage, colour, city and optional note) are sent to Mayank iPhone Stock by email using FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service.

The details are used to respond to your product enquiry and arrange follow-up about that request. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card details or other sensitive payment credentials.

Submitting the form is not an order and does not require payment. You can ask the business to stop contacting you about the enquiry.
